Built around the position

Training by position

Every player is rated on the key skills of their position. If we see a better fit, we train toward it and explain why.

Center back

Reading the play, heading, clean build-up with both feet, communication.

Full back

1v1 defending, recovery runs, timing the overlap, crossing on the run.

Defensive midfielder

Scanning, open body shape, line-breaking passes, switching play, covering.

Central midfielder

Receiving between the lines, turning, the final pass, arriving in the box.

Winger

Beating defenders 1v1, speed with the ball, cutting inside, finishing.

Striker

Runs in behind, holding up the ball, one-touch play, quick finishing.

12 weeks, 3 phases

The program

Weeks 1–4

Foundation

Bodyweight strength and core, clean movement, first touch and passing with both feet, scanning.

Weeks 5–8

Development

Bands and light load, acceleration and jumping, position-specific skills in small-sided games.

Weeks 9–12

Game transfer

Reactive speed and power, skills under pressure, real game decisions. Final progress check in week 12.

Parents ask

Questions

Does my player need to leave their team?

No. The program is built to complement team training. Home sessions are short and adjust around practices and games.

What equipment is needed at home?

Nothing special for the first month: a chair, a step and a backpack with some books. Later we add resistance bands, light dumbbells and a medicine ball.

Is it safe for growing kids?

Yes. We track height monthly, keep jump and sprint volume low, and reduce load during growth spurts or when there is soreness.

How do parents follow progress?

Through the reports at weeks 0, 6 and 12 and the parent meeting. Parents give consent before the app account is created.

Where do sessions take place?

Mondays and Wednesdays in Davie. Tuesdays and Thursdays in Miramar. We confirm the exact location when you book the evaluation.
